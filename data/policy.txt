HEALTHCARE SHIELD INSURANCE POLICY

Policy Name: HealthCare Shield Gold Plan
Policy Number: HCS-GP-2025
Coverage Start Date: 01-Jan-2025
Coverage End Date: 31-Dec-2025

1. COVERAGE DETAILS
The HealthCare Shield Gold Plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for hospitalization, diagnostic tests, and emergency care. 
The insured individual is eligible for:
- Inpatient hospitalization up to ₹10,00,000 per policy year.
- Pre-hospitalization expenses for 30 days prior to admission.
- Post-hospitalization expenses for 60 days after discharge.
- Coverage for ambulance charges up to ₹5,000 per incident.
- Cashless treatment at partner network hospitals.

2. ADDITIONAL BENEFITS
- Annual health check-up once every policy year.
- Maternity coverage after a 2-year waiting period.
- Newborn baby cover up to ₹50,000.
- Mental health consultation up to ₹10,000 per year.

3. EXCLUSIONS
The following are not covered under the HealthCare Shield Gold Plan:
- Cosmetic or plastic surgery unless medically necessary.
- Dental treatments except accidental injury.
- Non-prescribed vitamins or supplements.
- Pre-existing conditions during the first 2 years of the policy.
- Self-inflicted injuries or substance abuse.

4. CLAIM PROCESS
To file a claim:
- Notify the insurer within 48 hours of hospitalization.
- Submit required documents: claim form, discharge summary, bills, prescriptions.
- Claims are processed within 7 business days for cashless hospitals.
- Reimbursement claims take up to 14 working days post-document submission.

5. NETWORK HOSPITALS
The policyholder can access treatment from any of the 5000+ partner hospitals nationwide.
For a list of hospitals, visit www.healthcareshield.in/hospitals.

6. RENEWAL AND PORTABILITY
- Policy can be renewed annually before expiry.
- No medical check-up required for continuous renewals.
- Portability option available to switch to higher plans after 1 year.
